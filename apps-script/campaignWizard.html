Bulk import

One row per prospect in the "Bulk Import" tab — each row carries its own Sender (email/password/port), Campaign name, and full Initial + follow-up content. Rows that share the same Sender email + Campaign name are grouped together automatically, so 50 prospects for one campaign only creates that sender/campaign once, not 50 times. New senders still start Pending (same rule as guided setup) until their credentials are confirmed on the relay.

Step 1 of 4 — Sender

This password is never written to the Sheet. Submitting creates the sender as Pending (won't send yet) and gives you a copy-paste block below to hand off so the relay's real config gets updated -- that step happens outside the Sheet.

Step 2 of 4 — Campaign

Only campaigns for the sender you picked in Step 1 are listed. Prospects you add next go straight into this campaign -- nothing about it is recreated or changed.
24h "HH:mm". Use 00:00 / 23:59 to allow sending any time of day.

Step 3 of 4 — Prospects & templates

Sender and campaign are locked in from the steps above. Fill in one prospect's details and content below, click "Add Prospect", then repeat for the next one -- you never re-enter the sender or campaign.
